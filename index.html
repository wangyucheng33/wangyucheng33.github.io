About

I am a researcher at StepFun, working on post-training algorithms for foundation models with a focus on coding agents. Previously, I worked at ByteDance AI Lab and on Baidu's ERNIE post-training team, in both engineering and algorithm development roles.

I graduated from Fudan University with a bachelor's degree in mathematics after transferring from computer science. During my undergraduate studies, I gained some early research experience and wrote science fiction.

Before university, I was an OIer (now retired and completely replaced by AI).
