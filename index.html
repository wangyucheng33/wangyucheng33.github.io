About

I am a foundation model researcher at StepFun, where I focus on post-training for agentic models. Previously, I worked on the ERNIE team at Baidu and at ByteDance AI Lab. My research spans agentic language models, reinforcement learning, retrieval-augmented generation, and reliable long-form generation. I studied Mathematics at Fudan University and also write science fiction.
